# Platform icons

Apple and Google Play paths in `src/components/PlatformIcon.astro` come from
[Simple Icons 16.36.0](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/16.36.0),
licensed under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
Google Play's four path segments use its brand colors. The browser icon uses native SVG geometry.
The marks belong to their respective owners.

The site bundles these SVG paths locally. Unconfigured destinations retain their localized
coming-soon state; configured destinations retain their existing URLs and analytics events.
